What this does and does not change

The diagnosis from 28 Jul stands and does not need revisiting: an industry-wide Google update on 23–24 Jul, no manual action on any of the three, nothing to appeal. What the gate adds is that waiting it out has not worked.

One thing is still holding and should not be touched: ozem-plus.store is still position 1.42 for oz+ in Italy, improved from 2.29. Italy is the only place any of these three gained ground. Leave it alone.

The earlier "it is spreading" alarm was retracted on 28 Jul because it rested on incomplete days, and that retraction was correct at the time — 26 Jul really was RetaFit's best day. With 27–29 Jul now final, the decline is real. Same guard, opposite answer.

  2. Freeze ItalyThe single position that improved. No edits, no rebuilds, no link building anywhere near it.
