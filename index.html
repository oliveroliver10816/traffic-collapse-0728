Why the UK and Germany aren't coming back on these domains

This is the part that decides what we build next.

Look at who took our slots. fileybaypharmacy.co.uk/product/osanix/ — a real Filey Bay Pharmacy. stern-apotheke-frechen.de/produkt/ozem-plus/ — a real German pharmacy in Frechen. Plus about fifteen more genuine pharmacy and doctors'-practice domains.

They are running exactly our playbook — the same brand-review page, the same affiliate intent. They kept their rankings for one reason: their domains are real and old, and ours were 11 and 15 days old. When Google tightened, age and trust decided who stayed.

So the fix isn't better content — our content already beat them on Bing. The variable is the domain, not the page. Which leaves two honest options, and we should pick one deliberately rather than drift:

Build one durable domain that accumulates age and covers many brands, and stop minting a fresh shell per offer — or accept these are 2–4 week burn assets and require them to pay back inside three weeks. Right now we're paying the cost of the first and getting the lifespan of the second.

And one rule regardless: never let a site be 98% one brand word again. RetaFit outlived the other two by two days for exactly one reason — only 70% of its traffic was its own name. Concentration is what turned a ranking wobble into a total loss.
